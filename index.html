About

Jerstin Crosby, Untitled (Current Mood), 2018


The Yard was a project space and site for public art run by Jessica Langley and Ben Kinsley in their front yard in the Divine Redeemer neighborhood of Colorado Springs. 

Active from October 2017-July 2026, The Yard hosted 15 site-specific exhibitions by a roster of international artists. 

The Yard was a recipient of the INSITE Fund, administered by RedLine Contemporary Art Center and made possible by the The Andy Warhol Foundation for the Visual Arts and a 3×3 Projects grant from the Colorado Springs Fine Arts Center at Colorado College.

The Yard was an extension of “The Stephen and George Laundry Line” - a project space Jessica ran from an apartment in Queens, NY from May 2015-January 2017, which featured a monthly rotating presentation of work by artists, created specifically to hang from a third floor laundry line.
